Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

FitFlow Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use FitFlow Pro, we collect the following information:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register or sign in with Google
  • Fitness activity data — workout completions, running distances, durations, GPS route coordinates, and hydration logs
  • Device information — push notification subscription tokens for sending workout reminders
  • Usage data — which features you use and when, to improve the app experience

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalise your fitness tracking experience
  • To send daily workout reminder push notifications (only if you opt in)
  • To generate your weekly fitness report and progress stats
  • To allow admins to manage user accounts and app content
  • To improve app features based on usage patterns

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in Google Sheets via Google Apps Script, hosted on Google's infrastructure. We do not run our own servers. All data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS.

Some data is also stored locally on your device using browser localStorage for offline functionality.

4. GPS and Location Data

FitFlow Pro requests location access only when you start a running, walking, or cycling session. GPS coordinates are used solely to track your route and calculate distance. Location data is never shared with third parties and is only stored as part of your activity logs in your account.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Google — as our infrastructure provider (Google Sheets, Apps Script, Google Sign-In)
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect our rights

6. Push Notifications

If you enable push notifications, your browser's push subscription endpoint is saved to send you daily workout reminders. You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings or from within the app profile page.

7. Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. We do not receive your Google password or any other Google account data.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below. Account deletion is processed within 7 business days.

9. Children's Privacy

FitFlow Pro is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

10. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time
  • Export your fitness data on request

11. Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data including HTTPS encryption, secure API authentication, and access controls. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
